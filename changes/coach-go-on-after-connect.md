- **Coach asks a new sign-in only when a sign-in is the cause, and goes on after a connect (connect-an-app@32).** A failed grant whose reason isn't the sign-in (a tool the app doesn't list, an argument) is fixed in a new request reusing the same sign-in, never a new sign-in. Once a sign-in made for a request is connected, Coach goes on to that request's tools, rules and Card in the same reply, or says why not. Before, a Stripe grant failed on a tool's name and Coach asked the owner to sign in to Stripe again, and a Calendly connect ended at "signed in" with no taf drafted.
