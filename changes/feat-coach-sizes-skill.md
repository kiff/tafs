- **Coach sizes a new taf's job before drafting it (Coach, `set-up-a-taf@6`).** A new taf on a large list could page through it until its cost cap and stop with no answer. Coach now makes the check's read once itself first (its arguments, `limit 1`, the total where the vendor gives one), never hands over a check it hasn't seen read, and, with more than 50 items waiting or no total, writes bounded instructions (the 20 most recent, and the total) and tells the owner how many wait. After a first check that couldn't read, it never calls the taf ready or working. Reaches Coach with the next Upgrade, after its Eval (docs/coach.md).
