- **"Chase overdue invoices" takes Stripe as its billing app (tafs cloud).** Its first connection reads `quickbooks | zohobooks | moneybird | stripe`, and its words say "accounting or billing app" in all 8 languages, so a person who sends invoices through Stripe is offered it (Stripe and Gmail or Outlook already connect). The reminders go from the person's own email, never through Stripe, and the Card is unchanged: up to 20 a day, one a week per invoice, no edits, no credit note or discount (recipes/README.md).
