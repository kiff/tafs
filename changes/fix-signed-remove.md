- **tafs cloud: removing a taf is the phone's act.** `DELETE /v1/tafs/{id}` takes the phone's `KIFF-Device-Signature` with the approval key, behind the screen lock each time, checked by KIFF; a wrong signature or the device key is refused. Until both apps sign it, an unsigned removal still goes through and is logged, and `TAFS_SIGNED_REMOVE=on` then refuses it ([api.md](../docs/api.md)).
