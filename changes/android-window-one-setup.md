- **Android: an approval window is one setup's, and only its "Approve" is a tap.** A held window signs only the one call whose button said "Approve", which may confirm a new Card for the same taf. Another setup's card asks for the fingerprint and opens its own window. The window closes at once when the app leaves the foreground, as it does when the screen turns off.
