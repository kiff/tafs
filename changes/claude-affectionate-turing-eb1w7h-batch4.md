- **Eight more languages in the app:** Serbian (Latin), Albanian, Georgian, Armenian, Azerbaijani, Bengali, Hebrew and Arabic, on the iPhone, iPad, Android and the watch, with kiff.dev's pages for each (kiff/cloud#85, #87). Hebrew and Arabic read right to left. Serbian is the Latin script: a phone set to Serbian in Cyrillic keeps its next language. Written as they are, as the owner's rule of 22 Nov says: no native read holds a language back ([languages.md](docs/languages.md)).
- **Arabic: "Report this payment" and the glossary row for Setup.** The six `record.report.*` lines are written in Arabic, and `docs/languages.md` says how Setup is written (Latin letters for the agent, الإعداد for the two screen titles).
