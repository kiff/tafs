- **"Every refund asks you first" is understood in all 44 app languages.** When you agree rules that say every payment or refund asks you first, tafs now reads that in every language the app speaks, not only eight. It holds the taf to asking you each time, instead of letting payments under a figure go through by themselves. Rules that only ask above an amount ("every refund over €5 asks you") still let smaller ones run. See [Coach](docs/coach.md).
