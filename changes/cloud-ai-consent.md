- **Your data and the AI: nothing you write reaches the model before you allow it.** tafs cloud keeps each person's AI-data consent (`GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /v1/me/ai-consent`), with the screen's words in 8 languages: who (Anthropic), what's sent, what never is, and for how long. Until it's given, every route that starts a model turn for that person answers 403 `ai_consent_required` with those words. Runs with no person in the moment (a schedule, a door's message, an app's event, a hand-off, a retry, a task from Coach, a run after KIFF's alert) take the consent of the taf's maker, else the account's first yes from someone who manages its tafs, else a manager who took it over (`POST /v1/tafs/{id}/ai-consent`). Without it, the taf is paused with the reason, and what came in waits until it's back.
