- **Coach offers the hand-over tools only for a step that is the person's** (`a-browser-taf@3`). In the owner's own Chrome, a website taf may ask for `browse_hand` and `browse_hand_result`, and only for a step the person must do in the page (a card number, a check, a click the taf mustn't make). The taf hands that step over in its own words and reads their Done or Not now; it never does that step itself and never learns what they typed. Coach tells the owner the step shows as Your turn in the extension's panel. kiff/agents#653 names the same rule in planque's request.
