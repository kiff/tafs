- **Asking from the watch (tafs cloud, Android, Wear OS), off until it's switched on.** On the Wear OS watch, tap the microphone and say what you want: it goes to the one taf that can do it, under that taf's own Cards, and its result comes back as one short line, shown and spoken, with Hear and Reply. The phone signs each request with its device key, and only tafs cloud's watch route opens Coach's watch conversation, which has one skill, reads only the tafs the person may use, and hands the request to one taf at most. Nothing changes on the phone's screens or in Coach on the phone. Behind `TAFS_WATCH_ASK` ([docs/watch-ask.md](docs/watch-ask.md), [docs/watch-wear.md](docs/watch-wear.md)).
