- **iPhone and iPad: one Face ID, then taps, as on Android (the approval window).** When tafs cloud says a connection's approval can go in an approval window, one Face ID opens a five-minute window at KIFF, and the next approvals it covers are a tap: each is still signed on the phone, by the device key with the window's id, and KIFF and the connection server decide whether they take it. On a card tafs cloud marks a tap, KIFF's words ("No fingerprint for the next 4 minutes") stand under Approve while a window is held; anywhere else, Approve still asks for Face ID. Anything KIFF or the connection server refuses inside the window is signed again behind Face ID. The window closes after 30 seconds in the background, at once when the screen locks and before a drive, and is dropped at sign-out and at a new sign-in (docs/approvals.md, docs/ios-app.md). Coach's intro on iPhone and iPad no longer repeats that no taf sees your sign-in; the sign-in step says it.
