- **Improving a taf keeps its Cards' terms (tafs cloud).** When Coach improves a taf, it drafts the taf's Cards again from what KIFF holds. That copy dropped a total's `per` and the Card's start and end. So "one invoice per order, ever" came back as one invoice for the taf's whole life, and a Card that ended on a date came back with no end. Coach now gets every term a draft names: `per`, `valid_from`, `valid_until` and `location`. A Card's hold wait isn't copied, because a draft that leaves it out keeps the Card's own ([coach.md](docs/coach.md)).
