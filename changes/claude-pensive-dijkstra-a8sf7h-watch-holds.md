- **Apple Watch: a held call is on top of the Inbox, the moment it's held, and Approve on your phone opens it there.** The iPhone now tells the watch when its held calls change, so a new held call comes to the top of the watch's Inbox without opening anything, with how much waits for you. One answered on any device, or past its time, leaves. What goes to the watch is the taf's name and what the call asks, never its amount. Approve on your phone, on a held call, opens that call on the iPhone, in front or with a "From your watch" notification, to approve behind Face ID there; the watch never approves. See [the Apple Watch app](docs/watch-app.md).
