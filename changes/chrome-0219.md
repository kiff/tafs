Before, such a link could reach the site once. The taf is told `site_blocked` ([docs/chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)).
- Chrome end-to-end tests cover the riskiest open gaps: a sensitive site of the grant opens only on the phone's signed allowance, and a taf's click on a link to one the phone didn't allow asks it for nothing; "Not on this site" switched on mid-run; a step replayed after the service worker restarts; a click that would place an order or pay, known by the page's semantics alone.
