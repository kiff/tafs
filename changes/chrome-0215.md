- **Chrome 0.2.15: a taf's back and your tab.** A taf's Back after its own click goes to the page before it (Chrome had skipped it). In your tab, Back never goes to a page you had before the taf started, and a page off the taf's sites is blocked without blanking your page. Closing a taf's tab takes its site fence with it, a taf's next page after your tab is given back opens in a new group instead of failing, and a run's first page no longer fails now and then on a busy computer ([docs/chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)).
