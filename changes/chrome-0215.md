- **Chrome 0.2.15: a taf's back and your tab.** A taf's Back after its own click goes to the page before it (Chrome had skipped it). In your tab, Back never goes to a page you had before the taf started, a page off the taf's sites is blocked without blanking your page, and if your own Back leaves the taf's sites, your tab is simply given back to you. Pages you open yourself while a taf is paused are never read by it. Closing a taf's tab takes its site fence with it, a taf's next page after your tab is given back opens in a new group instead of failing, and a run's first page no longer fails now and then on a busy computer ([docs/chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)). The end-to-end tests now cover the fence's other ways off a taf's sites, no fence rule left behind (a restarted service worker included), your tab joined by a taf and your own Back in it, and masking in frames, with 5 `todo` tests left for what still goes wrong in your tab ([docs/chrome-e2e.md](../docs/chrome-e2e.md)).
