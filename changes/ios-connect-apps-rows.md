- **iPhone and iPad: Connect apps shows what each app is and where it stands.** Your connected apps come first with a ✓, then the rest to discover. Each row says how the app connects (sign in, or with a key), whose it is, and which tafs use it, with a compact Connect button. On an iPad it's a table. A connected app opens its detail, with Disconnect (docs/ios-app.md › Connect apps).
