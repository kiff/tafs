- **Android 1.8: Your step.** A step a taf may never take itself, such as approving a payout, comes first in Needs you in tafs cloud's words: what to do, why, and Open in {vendor} only when tafs cloud vouches for the link. Done and Skip it are signed on the phone over KIFF's `kiff.handoff.v1` and sent to KIFF, which tells the taf's run; answering grants nothing. Shown once tafs cloud turns Your step on (docs/android-app.md › Your step).
- **iPhone and iPad: Your step.** The same step shows first in Needs you, and on its taf's page in place of the request's line, in tafs cloud's words. Done and Skip it read the hand-off from KIFF for its version, are signed with the device key over `kiff.handoff.v1` (never the approval key), and go to KIFF; a step KIFF no longer waits on isn't sent. A button opens the vendor's page only for a link tafs cloud vouched for, its host shown under it, and the step's notification opens it in Needs you (docs/ios-app.md › Your step).
