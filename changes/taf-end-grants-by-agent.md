- Removing a taf ends its connections under every form of its agent's name: its KIFF agent, and the other form (with or without a generated suffix that has a digit) when every connection under it was asked for that same taf, never another live taf's own. The confirmation's "goes with it" reads the same agents, so what it says is what ends. A taf removed before this, whose connection was asked under the other form, had its Gmail connection left live.
- Connections a removed taf left behind are found on a person's read of the tafs list, at most once an hour per account: only agents KIFF reports archived for the account, or the other form of one asked on or before its archive day, never a live taf's, never one asked today or yesterday, and never an agent tafs cloud can't show was a taf. Until `TAFS_SWEEP_LEFTOVERS=on`, it ends nothing and logs the count; on, it ends them with the person's own session.
- An approval is never signed over a removed taf's connection, nor with a Card drafted for another taf than the connection's: it's answered at once, in 8 languages, and Coach hears to connect the app again for this taf. A new taf signed onto a removed taf's connection with one fingerprint on 14 Nov. A taf re-made under a removed one's name is its own: its connections, asked after the archive day, are never taken for the removed one's. A removal that can't read the account's connections fails that step rather than read as done.
