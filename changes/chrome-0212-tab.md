- **Chrome 0.2.12: a taf works in the tab you're on.** When you ask a taf from the side panel, the tab in front of that window joins its tab group and the taf works there, with Chrome showing the tab it works in while the panel is open; only on the sites you allowed, never a bank, payment, mail or health site, and only with your page consent. Stop gives the tab back, still open. A taf reuses its one tab group per window, and runs you didn't start from the panel open their own tab as before. It needs planque to name the run in each step before it takes effect ([chrome.md](docs/chrome.md#a-taf-works-where-you-are)).
