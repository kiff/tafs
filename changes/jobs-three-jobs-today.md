- **Three new jobs that use only apps that connect today (tafs cloud).** "Failed card payments" writes to a customer whose card payment failed, with the link to pay (up to 20 emails or drafts a day; it never charges, retries, refunds or cancels). "Payouts matched to the bank" matches each payout from the payment provider to its credit in the bank every morning and says what doesn't match; it only reads. "Late parcels" tells a customer when their parcel's tracking stalls or it goes back to the sender (up to 30 emails or drafts a day; it never buys a label or refunds). All three are in all 8 languages and listed by `GET /v1/recipes` now (recipes/README.md).
