- **Chrome: a taf never opens KIFF's own sites, merchant consoles are sensitive, and a click that sends money out is refused.** kiff.dev and its subdomains join the sites a taf never opens. Stripe, Paddle, Adyen, Square, Braintree and GoCardless join the sensitive sites, which open only when the paired phone allows one by name; the phones' list and `vectors/chrome-sensitive.json` match. A control or link whose whole label is a refund, a payout, a transfer or a withdrawal is refused as a payment step, in the same seven languages as the pay words ([chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)).
