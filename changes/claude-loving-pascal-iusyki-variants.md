- **iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android:** Brazilian Portuguese. A phone set to Brazilian Portuguese reads tafs in pt-BR ("Precisa de você", "celular", "Entrar"), and Portugal keeps European Portuguese. Lines that state a legal rule read as in Portuguese until they're reviewed ([languages.md](docs/languages.md)). Android's language list offers it as "Português (Brasil)", and its Terms and Privacy links open the Portuguese pages ([android-app.md](docs/android-app.md)).
