- **The check route checks the agreed rules in the conversation its caller shows.** `POST /v1/connect/grants/{grant}/check` takes `conversation`, the card's conversation's events in order, and checks the rules the owner agreed there as the chat's read does: a confirm skipped, a figure the notice's words don't say, an on_exceed off the rule. Those refusals are redraft ones, as on the phone. Without it the route still answers `rules_unchecked: true`. The Eval's e2e, which runs Coach's setup itself, shows its conversation, so it no longer signs a draft the phone would have refused.
- **The watch's answer stays one line (ask-a-taf@2).** Coach answers the watch with the taf's name and "working", no count of calls, and an ambiguous request with the tafs that could, no reasons: every answer at most 140 characters, the line the watch shows and says.
