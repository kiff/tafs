- **Plan & usage shows a store-billed period's end on the day the store says it renews (tafs cloud).** A period that ends mid-day, as an App Store, Google Play or Stripe period does at the time it was bought, read a day early ("ends on 26 Nov" where Apple's sheet says "Renews 27 November"). It now shows its end's own date; a period ending at midnight UTC still shows the day before. The apps draw tafs cloud's line, so no app release is needed.
