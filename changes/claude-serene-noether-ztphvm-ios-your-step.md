- **iPhone and iPad: Your step.** A step a taf may never take itself, such as approving a bank transfer, shows in Needs you and on its taf's page, in tafs cloud's words. "Done" or "Skip it" goes to KIFF, signed with the phone's device key over KIFF's `kiff.handoff.v1`, and the run goes on. A button opens the vendor's page only for a link tafs cloud vouched for, with its host shown under it. The step's notification opens it in Needs you (docs/ios-app.md › Your step).
