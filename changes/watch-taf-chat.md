- **Chatting with a taf from the watch (tafs cloud), off until it's switched on.** A watch request can name a taf the person chose (`taf_id`): its words go into that taf's own chat as the person, through the phone's own message path, signed by the phone's device key. Only a taf the person may use; a view-only phone is refused; a resent message never starts a second run. The taf's answer comes back as one checked line of at most 400 characters, with `more` when it goes on, and the whole answer on Read more (`?full=1`, read from the run on each tap, at most 16 KB, kept nowhere) and `held` when the taf waits on the person there. The watch never approves. Behind `TAFS_WATCH_TAF_CHAT` ([docs/watch-ask.md](docs/watch-ask.md)).
