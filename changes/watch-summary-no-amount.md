- **A Wear OS watch never keeps an amount:** the phone takes the call's own amount, and any figure with a currency beside it (€, zł, kr, PLN, euros…), out of what a held call wants before it goes to the watch, even where KIFF's title writes one in. Order numbers stay. The amount still shows in Needs you while the call is held ([watch-wear.md](docs/watch-wear.md)).
