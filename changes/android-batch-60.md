- **Android 1.10: your touch pauses Coach's drive.** While Coach drives the app, touching it pauses Coach at once, and your touch does what it always does. The caption reads **Paused**, with **Continue** and **Stop**. Continue puts the keyboard away, and Coach looks again before it acts. Going back, typing in a field, or a third tap on a control since the pause hands the app back to you. The drive card says so: "Touch the app to pause it, or tap Stop to end it." (docs/drive.md). Libraries updated: Kotlin 2.4, androidx.browser 1.10 and BouncyCastle 1.86.
