- **A card that says an app or its data is left unchanged is refused before anyone sees it.** Coach's wording rules (`contracts/coach-wording.json`) now catch "isn't changed", "stays as it is", "is left untouched", "leaves … unchanged" and "nothing is modified" in all 8 languages, so kiff/agents' `show` sends the card back with the rule's fix rather than leaving the core rule to the model alone. "Unchanged" as the job's own word ("it forwards each invoice unchanged to your accountant") still passes. Words by legal-i18n.
