- **The watch forgets an account whenever the phone's changes, not only on a sign-out it hears.** The phone puts a random account mark on everything it sends the watch, made at each sign-in and never derived from the account. A watch that sees it change, or that holds one and gets a reply without it, wipes its conversation, Inbox, kept message, cache and tile count before showing anything, and draws nothing it kept until the phone confirms the same sign-in. What the watch sends (a message, Refuse, Halt) carries the same mark, and a phone now on another account refuses it unsent. At sign-out the phone also tells the watch directly, so a watch that was off or out of reach forgets the account when it's back. Opened with the phone out of reach, the watch now shows its linking screen rather than the last read.
