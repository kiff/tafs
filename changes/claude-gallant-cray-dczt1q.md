- **French says "ce Mac" and "cet appareil" (iOS, Android).** French lines wrote their own "cet" or "ce" before the device's name, so a Mac read "cet Mac" and a phone "ce appareil". A line now writes `{device|this}` (`{device|This}` to open a sentence), and the apps pick "ce" or "cet" by the name, from one rule in `app/strings/generate.py` (`THIS`), as they do a possessive. Only French has the rule; a test refuses `|this` in any other language, and any French line that writes its own word before `{device}` (app/strings/README.md).
