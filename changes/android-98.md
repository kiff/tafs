- **Android 1.27.10 (98): a sign-in survives stepping away for a code, and its lines name the app.** The vendor's sign-in tab opens in tafs's own task, so coming back to tafs from Recents comes back to Google's page. Coming back with no link from it (from the launcher's icon, or after closing the tab) no longer closes the sign-in: the card offers "Open Gmail's sign-in again", the same link planque takes until its approve expires, and Cancel. Under a sign-in, "If Gmail's sign-in page opened, finish it there and come back" and "Opens Gmail's sign-in" name the app; "the vendor" only when no app is known (docs/android-app.md).
