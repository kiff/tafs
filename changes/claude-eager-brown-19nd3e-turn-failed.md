- **A failed turn has its own plain line, per reason, in all 26 languages.** When our AI provider can't answer (its balance, an outage, or the day's cap), the line says it's on our side and that none of the person's credits were used. Other lines cover the person's own credits, a timeout and an error, plus a Try again label. A turn that already changed something gets a line with no credit claim and no Try again, since sending it again could act twice. Words only; tafs cloud's failed-message route uses them.
