- **A refund cap that asks only past it is built as agreed, not refused.** Rules such as "up to €5 each, by itself; over €5 asks you first; past €50 a day it is refused" were refused when the €5 figure took the rule's "asks" instead of saying it on its own. Coach then told the owner such a cap couldn't be set. Those rules now give their two Cards (refused past €50 a day, held past €5 a refund). A refusal now names only what the rules need, and never says a rule can't be set. Rules whose words say every refund asks still need a count of 0 a day that asks. See [Coach](docs/coach.md).
