- **A door's taf that needs something from the owner says what (tafs cloud).** When a taf can't answer a door's writer because it needs something only the owner can give, it says `needs_setup` with any of a folder, a spreadsheet, a file, a link or some information, and the owner's row names them in tafs' own words ("File receipts needs something from you before it can do what Ben asked: a spreadsheet and a folder."). The taf's own sentence never reaches the row, so neither the writer's words nor internal ids do. The writer still gets nothing until the owner answers or ignores it.
