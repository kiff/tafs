- **A held purchase on Android (Android).** A purchase KIFF holds for the owner is drawn in KIFF's words: the shop, the total with the delivery address under it, the items behind Show more, the price rule, what the single-use card allows and when it buys. It's answered with Approve once, only from its own card, or Refuse; never Extra room, a next step or "Allow this from now on". Every answer signs the case's id, version and digest as its last line, byte for byte the contract's vector. See [Purchases](docs/purchases.md).
