- **tafs cloud: a refused signature says why, of this device.** When KIFF refuses a device's signature on an approval's report, a removal, a discard or a drive's start, the app now gets KIFF's reason as a code (`view_only`, `device_session_required`, `passkey_not_this_session`, `signed_at_out_of_range`, `signature_invalid`) with words in the person's language that say what to do, said of "this device", never "this phone". A view-only computer used to hear "this phone didn't sign that answer" (docs/api.md).
