- **Delete a Coach conversation on iPhone and iPad:** a swipe in Previous conversations or Archived offers to delete one for good, then asks again by name, in tafs cloud's words only (no Delete until tafs cloud sends them). The one on screen gives way to a new conversation, attachments opened from chats are cleared from the device, and the Apple Watch drops its kept copy of Coach's thread. One still working or waiting on a card stays, with tafs cloud's line ([docs/ios-app.md](docs/ios-app.md#deleting-a-conversation)).
