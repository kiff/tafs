- **Coach picks how a taf browses, and says why in tafs cloud's words (tafs cloud).** With both the cloud browser and the owner's Chrome on offer, Coach picks by the job (`a-browser-taf@9`). A taf that runs on a schedule or from a door, while the owner may be away, gets the cloud browser. One that needs the sign-ins already in their Chrome, or runs only when they ask, gets their Chrome. Coach sends a reason code (`schedule`, `door`, `signed_in`, `occasional`), never its own sentence, and tafs cloud refuses anything else. The taf's setup shows the reason ("Coach chose the cloud browser because File invoices runs at 08:00, when you may be away.") only while it's for the way the connection has. A version keeps its own pick, and the taf takes it once that version is installed.
