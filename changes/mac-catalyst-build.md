- **The app builds for the Mac (macOS 13 and later), in CI.** The iPhone and iPad app now also builds with Mac Catalyst, in the iPad's layout, and its unit tests run on a Mac (`.github/workflows/mac.yml`, the `TafsMac` scheme). A Mac build tells KIFF it's a Mac (`platform: macos`, kind `other`). It has no Live Activity and no Siri tip, which a Mac lacks. Nothing changes on iPhone or iPad, and there is no Mac release yet: the menu bar, Touch ID approvals on a Mac, adding a Mac from a phone, and signing follow (docs/mac-app.md).
