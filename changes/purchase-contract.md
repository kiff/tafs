- **The purchase contract (contracts/purchase.json).** One file every side of buying within the Card reads: the case and the digest that signs it (the delivery address included), its states (`unknown` among them), the decision and its reservation, the execution lease, the live authorization's rules (a timeout is a decline, one approval per case), the approval the apps draw in KIFF's words, the owner's signed answer and the signed receipt. Nothing is built on it yet; KIFF, planque and the apps build from it.
