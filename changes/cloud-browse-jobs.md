- **Jobs can be explored without creating anything (tafs cloud).** `GET /v1/recipes/gallery` sends the browse words, `explore` ("Explore jobs"), `see_all` ("See all jobs"), `try` ("Try it with Coach") and `sample` (the label above a job's report), in all 8 languages. Each recipe there and on `GET /v1/recipes` may carry `proved` ("Proved on a test help desk.") and `sample`, what the taf reported after its proof run. "Acknowledge every ticket" is the first to carry both, its sample being the taf's own report from its test run, in all 8 languages. `words.all` stays the heading inside the gallery; the row under the strip reads `see_all`. A recipe file names them as `proved:` and `sample:` (recipes/README.md, docs/api.md).
