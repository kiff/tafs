- **Coach sets up an app in two approvals** (connect-an-app@21, draft-the-card@14, name-the-tools@10). An app connected by its catalog entry gets Coach's own reads in its first request, approved with the sign-in. Then the taf's tools, set on the signed-in grant, come back declared, so Coach drafts the Card at once and shows one approval for the tools and the Card. Coach never offers a tool the connection lists as unusable (its sign-in can't run it), and says why in plain words. When the owner declines tools that were declared, Coach withdraws them. See [coach.md](../docs/coach.md).
