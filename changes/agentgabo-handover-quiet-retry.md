- **A taf's setup card no longer shows Coach's own mistakes in red.** When Coach wrote something wrong in a taf it was handing over and fixed it on its own, the card first showed a red technical line, even though the taf was set up a moment later. The card now shows "Setting up…" while Coach fixes it, then the taf once it's made. If it isn't made, the card says so in plain words and to ask Coach.
