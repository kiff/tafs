- **tafs cloud serves the watch's conversations by day (W11).** `GET /v1/watch/days` lists the days a person asked from their watch, newest first, with how many and today in the account's time zone; `GET /v1/watch/days/{date}` gives one day: what they said, where each went and its line, a reply under its request. Their own only, never another member's, the owner included; read only; kept as long as the conversation, 30 days (a watch request was kept 7). Behind the watch switch, so the iPhone and iPad's "From your watch" can be turned on ([watch-ask.md](docs/watch-ask.md#watch-days-w11)).
