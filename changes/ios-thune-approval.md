- **A held purchase on the iPhone and iPad (iOS).** A purchase KIFF holds for the owner is drawn in KIFF's words: the shop, the total and what it includes, where it's delivered, the items and charges behind Show more (closed each time), the price rule, what the single-use card allows and when it buys, and why the Card asks. It's answered with Approve once (behind Face ID, with KIFF's prompt naming the shop and the total) or Refuse, never Extra room, a next step or "Allow this from now on". Every answer signs the case's id, version and digest as its last line, byte for byte the contract's vector. A purchase whose words don't read is never approved: it's refused for its case, or answered in KIFF when its case can't be signed as sent. See [Purchases](docs/purchases.md).
