- **Apple Watch: nothing of one account stays under another.** The iPhone puts a random mark, made at each sign-in, on every reply and push to the watch. Another account's mark, or none once one was kept, wipes what the watch kept (threads, Inbox, a message kept for later, alerts) before anything is drawn, and a message kept under one account is never sent under another. At sign-in and sign-out the iPhone tells the watch at once and takes its own alerts away.
- **iPhone and iPad: Retry under a failed run only where tafs cloud says it's safe.** A taf's chat and Coach show the button only when the thread's `failed.retry` is true for that run, in its `retry_label`, so a run that acted part-way is never sent again with one tap.
