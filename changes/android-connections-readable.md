- **Android: your connections read plainly.** An app's connections show the connection service's name for each, who uses it and how it is, with no ids. A tap opens its detail, with only the actions that can work for it, and Remove asks once more, naming what stops. A refusal from the connection service, such as a sign-in again to another account, is said in its own words, never "It changed in the meantime" (docs/android-app.md). Android 85 (1.25.0).
