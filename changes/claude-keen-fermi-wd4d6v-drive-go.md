- **Coach goes straight to a screen while it drives the app** (drive-the-app@4). Coach's first look in a chat carries the app's map: the screens from app/routes.json this person may open, with their titles, and their tafs' ids. `app_act {action: "go", route}` then opens one by its route, and only opens it: tafs cloud refuses a route that isn't on the map, a taf of another account or not shared with the person, and a screen they may not open (one that needs managing that taf, or an account permission they lack), before anything reaches the phone. The apps open `go` in a follow-up; until then they refuse it as a step they can't do. See [drive.md](../docs/drive.md#going-to-a-screen).
