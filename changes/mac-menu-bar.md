- **Mac: a menu bar, and a smallest window size.** On the Mac build, the shortcuts that work on every screen are menu items: New taf (⌘N) in File, Account (⌘,) in the app's menu, and Tafs, Needs you, Record and Coach mode (⌘1, ⌘2, ⌘3, ⌘K) in View. The window never goes smaller than 820 × 600 points. Nothing changes on iPhone or iPad, and there is no Mac release yet (docs/mac-app.md).
