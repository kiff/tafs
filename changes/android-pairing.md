- **Android: pair a computer from Devices.** Account → Devices → Pair a computer scans the computer's QR, asks whether the computer shows the same 6-character check code, and on yes signs the pairing to KIFF with the approval key, behind a fingerprint that names the computer. KIFF decides whether it's paired; the computer is then listed with Sign out. Coach can't start or confirm a pairing (docs/android-app.md, Pairing a computer).
