- **One fingerprint for a key and its Card (Coach).** `connect-an-app@10` and `draft-the-card@8`: for an app connected by key, Coach sets the taf's tools on the grant while it waits for its key. Once the connection server says they're declared, Coach agrees the rules, drafts the Card and shows one approval, so the key sheet signs the key and the Card together. The sheet is the ask: there's no separate "may I connect?" card for a key. A grant that isn't declared goes key first, then Card, as before (docs/secure-connection.md).
