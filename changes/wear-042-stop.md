- **Wear OS, test versions: Stop now ends the recording before the words come back.** With the app's own microphone (Play and Pause, test versions only), Stop only asked the watch's recognizer to stop while the app kept sending it sound, so the recognizer never reached the end and the watch said "Didn't catch that". Stop now closes the recording first: the words come back, and the recording stays for Play ([watch-wear.md](docs/watch-wear.md)). Release versions, which keep no recording, are unchanged.
