- **Coach sets a taf's schedule with the taf, and its taf keeps what it makes** (set-up-a-taf@9). The schedule goes in the setup result, covered by the Card's approval, and never as a dial of its own while setting up. A schedule never reads something the taf's first run creates, such as its own sheet, so there's no failed first check and no version 2. Asking whether something the taf can make already exists, Coach offers "tafs creates it" as an answer. A taf's instructions say to keep the ids of what it makes and use them next time, rather than search by name, and to stop when a read is refused. See [coach.md](../docs/coach.md).
