- **Android 147 and Wear 0.4.10: held calls reach the watch, and the watch says what it did.** The phone pushes Needs you to the watch when a call is held or ends (`kept/needs-you`: the count, and each call's taf and what it wants, never an amount), so a held call shows at once at the top of the watch's Inbox, on the tile and as the watch's own alert, with Refuse behind the watch's lock and Approve on your phone; one that ended or was answered leaves. Open on phone shows "Opened on your phone", or "Check your phone" when it isn't reached. The linking screen waits half a second, so a phone that answers fast shows none of it. Home's "The update is ready" never offers a Restart that does nothing (docs/watch-wear.md, docs/android-app.md, "A newer version").
