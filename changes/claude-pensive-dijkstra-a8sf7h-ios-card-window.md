- **iPhone and iPad: the approval window is one taf's, and closes when the app leaves.** The window one Face ID opens now belongs to the taf whose step opened it: that taf's next approvals are a tap, and the card says so. Another taf's approval closes it and asks for Face ID, and its card names Face ID until then. The window now closes as soon as the app goes to the background, as well as when the screen locks. Widening a Card always asks for Face ID. ([approvals.md](docs/approvals.md), "One fingerprint, then taps")
