- **A turn that failed is always said, with why and whether to try again.** tafs cloud's conversation read gives every taf turn that ended `ERROR` its `failed: {run, code, words, retry, retry_label?}`. `code` is `ai_unavailable` (our provider, or its day cap), `timeout`, or `failed`, and `words` is the reason's line in the person's language (`turn_failed_*`). `retry` is true only when the run's record shows nothing it did that acted, so sending the message again can't act twice. A turn that acted partway says so, with no Try again.
