- **Chrome 0.2.19: a sensitive site under a taf's site is blocked by Chrome itself.** A bank, wallet, payment, health or government site the phone didn't allow, and Chrome's own pages, are now refused by Chrome's own rules for a taf's tab before any request is sent, even under a site the taf may use (bank.example.com under example.com). Before, such a link could reach the site once. An address with user info before the site's name is blocked too. The taf is told why, with the same code the extension's own check gives (`site_blocked`, `address_private`) ([docs/chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)).
- Chrome end-to-end tests cover the riskiest open gaps: a sensitive site of the grant opens only on the phone's signed allowance, and a taf's click on a link to one the phone didn't allow asks it for nothing; "Not on this site" switched on mid-run; a step replayed after the service worker restarts; a click that would place an order or pay, known by the page's semantics alone.
- Chrome end-to-end tests check each kind of host the new fence blocks: a bank label under a taf's site, Chrome's own pages
  under a granted `google.com`, a site under a listed public suffix, and an address. Each is tried by an open, by a link and
  by a redirect, in a taf's own tab and in yours. Chrome asks none of them for anything. Other tests check that a page cut to
  fit the relay, or a hand-over's Done sent after the relay comes back, carries nothing masked or typed.
- **Chrome 0.2.20: a taf never acts on what a page made unusable.** A field or button the page disabled (a disabled fieldset included), one inside an inert part of the page or marked as disabled, a read-only text field, and a disabled choice of a list are refused, and a taf can't "uncheck" a radio ([docs/chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)).
- Chrome end-to-end tests cover the rest of the ranked gaps: controls the person can't use; planque's stop mid-act; a page's dialogs as it loads (pinned as they are today, an open gap), and "leave this page?"; which senders may command the extension; the floating bar and the badge after the person takes their tab back.
