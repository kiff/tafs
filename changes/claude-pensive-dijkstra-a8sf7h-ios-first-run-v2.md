- **iPhone and iPad: the first-run cards are four, and an empty Home suggests a first taf.** The cards are now Your tafs work for you, Coach sets it up with you, A Card is your permission (ending with this device's own way to approve), and Credits pay for the work, each drawn with the app's own views and the sample's words; a swipe moves between them. With no taf, Home leads with A first taf to try: the job tafs cloud names in `home.first_taf` (a proved recipe in the person's language), and Set it up with Coach puts its words in Coach's box. With no such job there's no card. See [Onboarding](docs/onboarding.md).
