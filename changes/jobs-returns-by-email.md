- **A new job, "Returns by email" (tafs cloud).** For small shops that take each return by email or form, check it by hand and refund days later: the taf answers return requests with the shop's return steps, and refunds a return once the owner marks it received in the shop. Its Card allows replies up to 30 a day and refunds up to 80 EUR each and 400 EUR a day, back to the original payment; anything more waits for the owner, and it never edits or cancels an order. In all 8 languages. It connects an inbox or help desk and the shop's store app, so like the other store-app jobs it stays out of `GET /v1/recipes` until that app is proved to connect (rule 13), then shows with no other change (recipes/README.md).
