- **An approval card's answer comes settled:** each answered approval in a chat's read now carries `answer`, free of any language: `approved`, `already approved`, `connected` (a connect step that worked, its Card next), `declined` or `failed: <why>`. The apps draw "approved" only from the first three, never from the words, so a refusal can't read as approved in any language (the owner's Reject on Windows read "Approved · signed in to Stripe", 17 Nov).
