- **Android: a locked phone or watch shows only "A taf needs you".** tafs cloud's alert words can name an amount, a merchant or a person, and Android showed them on the lock screen, and on a Wear OS watch the phone bridges alerts to, as they came. Every alert is now private, with "A taf needs you" as its public version, on every channel, as iOS's hidden previews; the details show once the phone or watch is unlocked (docs/android-app.md).
