- **iPhone and iPad: the Record searches as you type, and Answer within reads in days.** As on Android 1.7.2. The Record's box searches what's typed 400 ms after the last key, so a word typed letter by letter is one search; the Search key still searches at once, and an emptied box shows the Record again. Answer within's 24, 48 and 168 hours read "1 day", "2 days" and "7 days" in every language (the shared `work.answer_within_days`), and are still sent to tafs cloud as hours (docs/ios-app.md).
