- **Connect apps: an app whose key goes with your own login at the vendor (Zendesk) asks for your email first, on iPhone and iPad, and connects with it.** tafs cloud lists such an app with `login: true` and starts it with the email the app sends as `login`; one that isn't one email address is refused (`login_invalid`) and nothing starts.
