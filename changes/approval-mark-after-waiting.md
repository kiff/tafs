- **An approval card's mark is what its tap asks while another app waits:** when two apps' approval cards arrive in one read, the first card was marked to sign the Card while its tap, with the other app still waiting, only connects. The phone refused that step as not its mark, so the owner tapped again and again. The mark now reads the waiting apps the same way the tap does (`stepWithWaiting`), and a card answered as a split approval loses its mark, so the phone shows it answered instead of offering a tap that gets 409.
