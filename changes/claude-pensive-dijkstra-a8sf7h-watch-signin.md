- **Apple Watch: signing a watch in again after "Sign out on this watch" needs a yes on your iPhone.** The watch's button now reads "Ask your phone to sign this watch in". Your iPhone asks "Sign this watch in as you?", and Allow works only once the iPhone is unlocked. Deny, no answer, or an answer that comes too late leaves the watch signed out. Nothing from before the sign-out comes back. See [the Apple Watch app](docs/watch-app.md).
