- **The Card step on iPhone and iPad:** before Coach's first approval, Start's screen says what the approval allows, what always waits for you, how it signs and how to stop it. Then comes the plain list of Coach's tools with their daily limits, and KIFF's own words for the Cards under "Exactly what you sign". The button is "Approve with face" or "Approve with fingerprint". The list is checked against what's signed, field by field, and nothing is signed on any difference. Coach takes no typing until its tools are approved ([docs/ios-app.md](docs/ios-app.md#the-card-step)).
