- **iPhone and iPad: an app's key is given on its own secure sheet, never in Coach's chat.** When Coach asks for an app that connects with a key, its card has one button, "Give the key on a secure sheet". The sheet opens full screen and shows the app, what it may read or write, and where the key goes: from your phone to the connection server, which sends it only to that app, and never to Coach. Face ID confirms there, and the clipboard is cleared after you paste (docs/ios-app.md).
