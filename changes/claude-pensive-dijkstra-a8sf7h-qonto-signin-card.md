- **Coach: a vendor's own sign-in is asked alone, its guide's lines are on the card, and it never falls back to a key.** When an app's guide says what to tell the owner before its sign-in, those lines are now the text of the card that asks to go ahead, so the card never asks about lines the owner can't see. A vendor's own sign-in entry (Qonto, Stripe) is asked by its entry alone, with no tools and never with its server too; a request refused for its shape is corrected once; and a vendor's own sign-in that still fails is never replaced by its key: Coach says the sign-in couldn't start and stops (`connect-an-app@37`). See [Coach](docs/coach.md).
