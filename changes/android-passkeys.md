- **Android 1.30.0: a passkey on this phone.** Account → Devices → Passkeys lists the account's passkeys, and "Add a passkey on this phone" makes a kiff.dev passkey through Android's Credential Manager and registers it with KIFF, signed by the approval key behind the fingerprint. A computer that approves with a passkey can then approve through this phone, by its QR code. Removing one is signed the same way, and a computer's own passkey goes with its computer (docs/android-app.md).
