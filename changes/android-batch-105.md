- **Android 1.29.4: an approval card no longer stays on "Reading what it asks for…", Open again runs the approval again, and a removal on its way says so.** A card whose grant planque listed more than nine seconds late read the grants again every three seconds until listed or answered, for ten minutes at most, where it read them three times. Open again on a sign-in left no longer reopens the spent link: it runs the card's approval again, with one fingerprint, so a sign-in planque holds is confirmed with no page and any other gets a fresh link. While access is being removed, Connect apps and the connection's button say "Removing access…" instead of "Connected ✓", and no second removal starts. A grant that doesn't match its digest writes one logcat line saying which way it failed, with its status and `digest_v` only. kiff.dev now vouches for the app, so its passkeys can be kiff.dev's once the app makes them; Android needs no change in the app for it (docs/android-app.md).
