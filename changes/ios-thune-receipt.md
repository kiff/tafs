- **A purchase's receipt in the Record (iOS).** A purchase's line opens KIFF's signed receipt in its sheet, checked on the iPhone or iPad against KIFF's receipt key built into the app (`kiff-receipts-1`), never one fetched with it. A checked receipt says what was bought as its headline; one that can't be checked says so first, with KIFF's words muted under it; one that was changed, or is another case's, shows only that it doesn't check out. The order's line is `order`, or `shipping` on a receipt issued before. See [Purchases](docs/purchases.md).
