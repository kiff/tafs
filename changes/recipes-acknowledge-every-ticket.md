- **A new ready-made job: Acknowledge every ticket (tafs cloud).** Every hour, the taf finds the help desk's new tickets. It sends each one a short acknowledgement (the message arrived, and when it will be answered), once per ticket and up to 50 a day, and labels it by topic. Any other reply waits for the owner, and it never closes or deletes a ticket or promises a refund. It's listed in Customers, in all 8 languages, for any help desk. It's the first job whose Eval case passes on a test account, built from these words alone (recipes/README.md).
