- **Chrome 0.2.21: a taf's click that ran is never reported as failed because its page grew too large to send.** It's reported as done, and the taf reads the page again, so it never clicks Submit twice. Only opening or reading a page that's too large is refused ([docs/chrome.md](../docs/chrome.md)).
- Chrome end-to-end tests cover the last gaps that touch your data: Coach's own send starting a run in your tab; Coach's read of a page with frames; a page too large to send even when cut; a click whose page then grows too large, answered as done; and a Submit whose answer is too large, clicked once, posting once.
