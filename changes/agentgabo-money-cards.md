- **Money tools' Cards are built from the agreed rules.** For a tool that moves money, tafs cloud builds the Cards from the rules the owner agreed, and Approve answers any other draft with the exact Cards to draft instead. A refund that "waits for you" and is "refused over €50" now gets a held Card that carries the same €50 cap, so KIFF signs it, and a refund over €50 is still refused; the app lists that cap once. Coach no longer says it's waiting for an approval that was refused, and when asked what was refused, it quotes the refusal's own words. See [Coach](docs/coach.md).
