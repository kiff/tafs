- **"Your step": a step a taf hands its owner (tafs cloud).** A taf that prepared a step it may never take itself (approving a transfer, signing) hands it to its owner with kiff/agents' `your_step` tool. Home lists each step waiting as `your_steps`, with what, why, the vendor's https link and its host (only for a host the taf's own connections vouch for; else the words alone), and tafs cloud's words in the person's language ("Your step", "Done", "Skip it", "Open in {vendor}"). Needs you counts it, and the taf's request reads as the owner's step, never as an approval. The phones that answer hear once when it starts waiting and once a day later. The apps answer KIFF directly with the phone's signature. It's off until `TAFS_YOUR_STEP=on` (docs/api.md › Your step).
