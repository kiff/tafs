- **iOS, Android: Devices names each device in the app's language.** A device signed in from the app in another language read "tafs en iPad" or "tafs sur Android appareil" among English ones. The apps now tell KIFF what the device is (iPhone, iPad, Android phone or tablet), and Devices shows KIFF's name for it in the app's language; a device's own name shows only for one of another kind (docs/accounts.md › Devices).
