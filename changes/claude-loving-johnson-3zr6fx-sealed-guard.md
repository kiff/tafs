- **iPhone and iPad: a held sign-in fill in the owner's Chrome offers only Refuse.** Allowing a fill takes a release from the phone's approval key, which the iOS app can't make until its release card exists. So a hold whose browser step is a sign-in fill (`sealed_fill` or `fill`) shows "This fill can't be read, so it can't be allowed. You can still refuse it." and no Approve once, Extra room or "Allow this from now on". A block that can't be read counts as a fill. Nothing changes for any other hold.
