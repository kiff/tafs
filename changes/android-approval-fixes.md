- **Android:** an approval card's button now does what it says. "Approve with your fingerprint" asks for the fingerprint even while an approval window is held; only a card that says "Approve" goes as a tap. A sign-in left on its vendor's page no longer greys every other approval card until tafs is opened again: approving another card takes over from it, and a card held by a sign-in still under way says why ([android-app.md](docs/android-app.md)).
