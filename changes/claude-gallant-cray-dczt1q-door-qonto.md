- **A door's reply for a run stopped at its turn or cost limit says it couldn't finish, even when it wrote something (tafs cloud).** Before, a run cut off after writing a step on the way ("Now let me check order 43.") sent that step as its reply; now it sends "I couldn't finish this. My owner can see what happened in the tafs app." A run that answered sends its answer as before (docs/doors.md).
- **Connect apps' rows carry the app's name and a subtitle (tafs cloud).** Each row now has `name`, what it reads as ("Qonto", "Gmail"), and, for a reviewed entry whose title says more, `subtitle` ("Invoicing: reads, and invoice drafts from quotes"). `title` is unchanged, so the apps draw the long title until they switch to `name` and `subtitle` (docs/api.md).
