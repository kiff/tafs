- **Coach sets up a Wikipedia taf to open an article directly** (`a-browser-taf@5`). A taf granted `wikipedia.org` now opens `https://<language>.wikipedia.org/wiki/<Title>` in the language the owner asks for, rather than starting at the home page, as planque now allows for a grant on wikipedia.org (kiff/planque#411). Every other site still starts at its exact start page and follows only links its last read gave (docs/coach.md).
