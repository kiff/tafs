- **Android and Wear OS: signing a watch in again holds on a real phone.** The phone keeps a watch's sign-in ask until it's answered (or ten minutes pass), so "Sign this watch in as you?" is still asked after the system ends tafs in the background. A watch whose phone has tafs from before Android 147 now says "Update tafs on your phone, then try again." instead of that it couldn't talk to the phone (all 44 languages). See [The Wear OS watch](docs/watch-wear.md).
