## When to use

Answers about the plan, credits, packs and what's left, in Plan & usage's words. Use when the owner asks about cost, a plan, credits, a pack or what is left.

## Procedure

When the owner asks about cost, the plan, credits, packs or what's left, say it as
kiff.dev and Plan & usage say it:

- **Credits.** A taf's run uses credits for the AI it used, in whole credits,
  counted once when the run ends. A quiet check, when nothing is new, costs
  nothing. A trial of a new version, and a run that failed on our side, aren't
  counted. A run whose call KIFF refused still used its AI, so it counts.
- **Solo**, for one person: €12.50 a month, VAT included, with 500 credits each
  period. It's bought in the tafs app, through the App Store or Google Play,
  renews every month until cancelled, and is cancelled in the store's
  subscriptions. There's no free trial: an account pays from its first day.
- **Packs** add credits on Solo and never expire. They're bought in the tafs
  app, through Google Play or the App Store, once Solo runs. The First month
  pack is bought once per account, on Google Play only. The period's credits
  are used first, then packs. Their sizes and prices are the ones Plan & usage
  shows.
- **Company**, for a team: €10.50 a seat a month, plus VAT, with 500 credits a
  seat, pooled. Past them, €0.01 a credit, on the account's invoice, up to the
  spend limit an admin sets.
- **Warnings.** At 80% the owner is told. At 100% on Solo, the tafs wait until a
  pack adds credits or the period starts again. On Company, the tafs wait at the
  spend limit until an admin raises it.
- **Coach.** Coach is included: enough to set up about 4 new jobs a month. Past
  that, Coach's help uses your credits.
- **Asking.** A member who can't buy asks an admin for a pack or a higher spend
  limit, with a reason. The admin buys it, raises it or declines.

Say only the numbers Plan & usage shows for this account (what's used, what's
left, the period's end, the limit), never ones you work out, and never turn an
AI cost into credits or euros. When a number isn't shown there, say where the
owner sees it: Plan & usage. Never buy, request or approve a plan, a pack or a
raise.

## Verification

What this skill says is done only when the owner saw it: the card shown, the result or version answered by tafs, the grant's status read. Nothing is done because it was said.
