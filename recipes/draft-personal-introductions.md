Each weekday morning, read my list of companies in my spreadsheet. For each one I haven't written to yet, write a draft in my email: a short personal introduction that uses the reason I noted for that company, and nothing I didn't note. One draft per company, up to 10 a day. Never send anything yourself: every send waits for me, and I approve each recipient and each message. Skip anyone I already wrote to and anyone marked as not wanting to be contacted, and tell me who you skipped and why.
