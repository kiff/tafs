title: Turn an order into an e-invoice
category: money
order: 7
line: Builds the client invoice from an order or timesheet in your email or files; you approve each one before it's finalised and sent.
headline: Invoice the day the order comes in.
today: An invoice typed by hand goes out late, and a late invoice is paid late.
connections:
  - gmail | outlook | googledrive
  - qonto
card:
  allows:
    - Draft client invoices, from an order or a timesheet, up to 20 a day
    - Add a client the order names, or update its details
  holds:
    - Finalising and sending an invoice, each one
  never:
    - A payment or a transfer
    - Cancelling or deleting an invoice
    - A discount the order doesn't have
---
When an order or a timesheet arrives in my email or my files, draft the client invoice in my business account, with the client, the lines and the amounts as the order gives them. Add the client if it's new. Ask me before finalising and sending each invoice, and never make a payment, cancel an invoice or add a discount. Tell me which orders you couldn't read.
