Version 2 of tafs on Wear OS, for testing.
- Coach is home: speak, type or pick a quick reply, check your words, then send.
- Play your recording before sending. It stays on the watch, and is erased once sent or deleted.
- Keep a message for later when your phone is out of reach.
- An Inbox for answers and held calls, until you dismiss them.
- Each taf's page and settings, and choose which tafs to halt.
- Today's calendar, and your account. Approving stays on your phone.
