The tafs app for Wear OS, a companion to the tafs phone app.
- Ask your tafs from your watch: speak, and hear the result.
- Needs you and Your tafs on your wrist, read from your phone.
- Refuse a held call, or halt a taf, from the watch. Approving stays on your phone.
- When nothing needs you, a green check says so.
- A tile to ask tafs, with how many need you while the watch is unlocked.
