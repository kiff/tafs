The first tafs app for Wear OS, a companion to the tafs phone app.
- Needs you and Your tafs on your wrist, read from your phone.
- Refuse a held call, or halt a taf, from the watch. Approving stays on your phone.
- A tile with how many need you, shown only while the watch is unlocked.
- Talk to Coach: it gets your words as text, never your voice.
