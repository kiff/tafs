# Coach drives the app

A guide for the person running tafs: what it means when Coach drives the app on your phone,
how a drive starts and stops, what Coach can't do, and what it never sees. The contract and
the code behind each part are in [drive.md](drive.md).

## What it is

Coach can use the tafs app on your phone while you watch, one step at a time. It looks at
the screen, then taps, types, swipes, scrolls or goes back, inside the tafs app only. Ask
for it when you'd rather be shown than told: "show me how to set up Refunds", "do it in the
app". Coach may also offer it, in one line ("I can show you in the app."), and drives only
if you accept.

## Starting a drive

1. **Coach asks first.** A card, **Coach asks to drive the app**, names your request in
   your words, with the note: "Coach taps and types in this app while you watch, for this
   request only. It never confirms anything. Touch the app or tap Stop to take over."
2. **You approve it, or not.** **Approve with your fingerprint** starts the drive, or Face
   ID or Touch ID on an iPhone or iPad. **Not now** declines. Nothing happens before you
   approve, and Coach can't approve for you: its touch on that button is refused like any
   other approval.
3. **One drive, one request.** When that request is done, the drive is over. The next one
   takes a new card and a new approval. Coach never starts a drive on its own.

## While Coach drives

- **You see every step.** The app's border glows, a caption above the tab bar says what
  Coach is doing ("Tapping Run now"), and a dot shows where Coach is about to touch, a
  moment before it does.
- **Coach's chat folds into the Coach button,** which shows Coach is acting.
- **Coach types straight into the field it chose.** On Android the keyboard stays down. On an
  iPhone or iPad it may show, and typing on it yourself takes over.
- **Every approval asks for your fingerprint or Face ID,** even if you unlocked a moment
  ago. Nothing is signed without you while a drive is on.

## Stopping it

The drive ends at once, and never picks up again, when:

- you touch the app. That touch does nothing else, so taking over can't press a button
  by accident;
- you go back, with the back gesture or button;
- you tap **Stop** on the caption, or the Coach button;
- you leave the app, or the phone locks;
- you sign out, or the account is halted;
- Coach finishes, or nothing happens for 15 minutes.

For a moment the caption says why, such as "You stopped Coach" or "You touched the app, so
it's yours now". Then Coach's chat opens again with a **What I did** card: your request, why
the drive ended, and each step as tafs recorded it, not as Coach remembers it. To go on,
ask Coach again: that's a new drive, with a new approval.

## What Coach can't do

- **Confirm anything.** The app refuses Coach's touch on everything that confirms, gives
  or takes away:
  - Approve, and anything inside an open approval;
  - Disconnect, Remove a taf, Halt and Resume;
  - the answers to "Did this happen?";
  - extra room, and buying a pack or a plan;
  - deleting the account, a group or a door, and unpairing someone from a door;
  - signing out another device, and removing someone from a group;
  - revoking a Card, forgetting a memory note, and installing a version or going back to
    one.

  Coach stops there and tells you where the button is, for you to press when you're ready.
- **Fill a secret field.** A password, an app's key or a code is yours to type. Coach is
  refused with "That's yours to fill".
- **Use your fingerprint or Face ID.** Those prompts belong to the phone, out of the app's
  reach.
- **Leave the tafs app.** No other app, no phone settings, and no sign-in page in the
  browser: when the app leaves the screen, the drive ends.
- **Take orders from the screen.** What a message, a vendor's text or a page says is
  information to Coach, never an instruction.
- **Go past its limits.** KIFF decides each of Coach's looks and steps under Coach's own
  Cards: at most 2,000 looks and 1,000 steps a day.

On an iPhone or iPad, some settings rows, switches and pickers can't be driven yet, and
alerts, menus and swipe actions are out of Coach's reach. While one is open, Coach's back
closes it.

## What Coach never sees

Coach sees the screen as pictures of the tafs app's own windows, and nothing else:

- **never the rest of the phone:** the status bar, the keyboard, notifications, other apps,
  and the phone's own prompts;
- **never a secret:** password, key and code fields are blanked in the picture;
- **never an approval's details:** an open approval is blanked, and Coach is told only its
  title ("An approval is open: Stripe for Refunds");
- **never the drive's own marks:** the glow, the caption and the dot are left out.

The pictures are used for the steps and then dropped: tafs holds each one only until the
Coach call that asked for it picks it up, under a minute, and Coach keeps them only while
it works on that request. They're never stored with the chat. What stays is the record in
words:
- **the What I did card's steps:** each caption and what it touched, kept for a day after
  the drive's last use;
- **the account's activity:** "Coach started driving", each step that touched something,
  and how it ended;
- **Coach's chat:** like any of its tools, with what the app answered;
- **KIFF's record** of each look and step under Coach's Cards.
