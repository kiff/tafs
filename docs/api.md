# tafs cloud API

tafs cloud is the phone's API: an AWS Lambda in eu-west-1 at
`https://cloud.tafs.kiff.dev`, with CloudFront in front of its function URL. A chat's
streamed answer comes straight from the streaming function's URL. The code is
`runtime/src/cloud/`: `handler.ts` routes every request (`route`), `handleStream` serves
the two streams, and `lambda.ts` wires the stores.

## Who is calling

- **A person:** `Authorization: Bearer <KIFF session>`. tafs cloud asks KIFF who that is
  (`whoIs`): the account, the person, and their capabilities. A KIFF agent key is refused
  (403, "sign in as a person, not with a key"). tafs cloud keeps no accounts of its own.
- **Only the account's tafs:** another account's taf answers exactly like a missing one
  (404, `owned`).
- **Managing a taf** (marked *manages* below) takes `cards.manage` and KIFF saying the
  person manages that taf (`needTaf`, `GET /v1/me/access` on KIFF). An editor manages
  only the tafs they made.
- A few routes take no sign-in; each says what checks it instead.

**The person's language:** the apps send their own resolved languages as `Accept-Language`, as
they do to KIFF. tafs cloud writes the lines it words for people in the best of its eight languages,
else English ([languages.md](languages.md)): so far a taf's work lines and requests (`…/work`, the request
page, the home list's line), the skipped firings, and a run code's `words` (`ai_unavailable`) on a chat's
`end`. The rest of its lines follow. An `error` stays in English; `words` beside it are the person's.

Capabilities are KIFF's: `cards.manage`, `holds.answer`, `members.manage`,
`billing.manage`, `account.delete` ([accounts.md](accounts.md#roles-and-what-a-person-may-do)).

**Errors** are JSON, `{"error": "<words for a person>"}`, sometimes with more fields
(a version switch blocked by a run names it: `blocking`). A refusal kiff/agents gave carries
its `code` (`credits_used_up`, `limit_reached`, `no_plan`, `halted`, `switching`, `run_changed`)
and `words`, what it means and what to do, for the app to show as they are: a refusal for credits
says what lets the tafs go on, and when the period starts again
([accounts.md](accounts.md#the-balance-read)), and a `halted` one says, in the person's language, whether the whole
account is halted (a Coach chat's refusal included) or the taf on its own, and how to resume it. 404 also covers what isn't the
caller's; 409 a state that doesn't allow it now; 429 a flood guard; 502 KIFF or kiff/agents
not answering. An unexpected error is 500, "something went wrong on tafs cloud", and the
details go only to tafs cloud's own log.

## Routes

### The account

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /v1/me/suggestions`, `PUT /v1/me/suggestions` | a person; manages, to change | The account's "Show suggestions" setting, kept in tafs cloud: `{suggestions: {show, reason: on | off | live, words}}`. On by default; `PUT {show: false}` turns them off for good (`{show: true}` on again); 403 for someone who doesn't manage the account's tafs. `words` are tafs cloud's for the apps to show beside the switch |
| `GET /v1/me/ai-consent`, `POST /v1/me/ai-consent`, `DELETE /v1/me/ai-consent` | a person | Their own AI-data consent ([accounts.md](accounts.md#your-data-and-the-ai)): `{consent: {given, at?, version, words: {title, lines, allow, not_now, withdraw, given}}}`, the words in their language. `POST {version}` allows it for the version they read (409 with the current words when it isn't the current one); `DELETE` withdraws it. Not a signed call: it grants no authority. `DELETE` keeps the record, marked withdrawn. Until it's given, every route by which they start a model turn or let one go on (a chat message, streamed or not, a card's answer, a steer, Run now, a trial and its messages, Improve, "Not sure, help me check", an approval card's ask and report, a held call's continue, a drive's start, results and resume) answers 403 `{error, code: "ai_consent_required", consent}`, and nothing reaches the model. A manager's yes is also the account's first yes, which its tafs from before take for their runs with no person in the moment; a yes or a withdrawal pauses or resumes the tafs that take it at once |
| `POST /v1/tafs/{id}/ai-consent` | someone who manages the taf | They take its runs with no person in the moment over with their own consent ([accounts.md](accounts.md#your-data-and-the-ai)), which they've given (403 `ai_consent_required` otherwise). A taf paused for consent goes on, and what waited starts. Answers `{taf}`. A taf paused for consent shows `consent_paused {why: "no_one" \| "not_given", words}` wherever it's read |
| `GET /v1/jobs` | anyone | The five starter jobs, kept for the tafs that have one: `{jobs: [{id, name, summary, cron}]}`. A new taf carries its own job (`POST /v1/tafs`), and the five are recipes (`GET /v1/recipes`) |
| `GET /v1/home` | a person | The app's list: each taf's state, colour, Card summary (a draft's KIFF Cards shown as one in `card.cards`, with `ids`, [tafs.md](tafs.md#the-home-list)), its `last_change`, its `health` (`{level, lines, fixes?}`, [reliability.md](reliability.md#its-health); left out when it can't be read in time), its `oldest_waiting` (`{since, words, late}`, only when a case waits) and `promise` (`{hours}`, only when its owner set one) ([reliability.md](reliability.md#its-oldest-waiting-case)), its `job_summary` (what Coach said it does, else its job's line), its `last_run` (its own latest, scheduled or from its chat, never a trial's), doors (each with its `label`) and open trial, and `setting_up`, the tafs whose setup stopped partway, each with the name Coach gave it and, for the person who started it only, `finish: {taf, session, words}`, the Coach chat that finishes it, and `discard: {cards, words: {button, title, text, yes, no}}`, to drop it (`cards`: the ids of the Cards in force on its agent, as a live taf's `card.ids`, for the phone to revoke first) (`POST /v1/home/setting-up/{kiff_agent}/discard`, below) ([tafs.md](tafs.md#the-home-list)); `others` repeats them for apps from before `setting_up`, and an agent made through KIFF's API is never listed. Also `limits: {text, trial_idle_days}`, the longest message and how long a trial waits unused, which the apps check and keep no copy of. `needs_you: {count, words}` is what Needs you lists, counted once so Home, the tab's badge and the lock screen agree; `words` says it in the person's language ("Nothing needs you.", "4 need you."), and it's left out when KIFF's "Did this happen?" can't be read. `headline: {words}` is Home's first line, counted from the tafs listed: how many are at work ("2 tafs working."); with none, how many are ready to work from their chat, a taf with no schedule ("1 taf ready."); else "0 tafs working.". A taf waiting on a connected app's event, or with something gone wrong (`work.attention`), counts as neither. The apps draw it and count nothing themselves. |
| `POST /v1/home/coach-waiting/{session}/set-aside` | the person whose Coach chat it is | Sets a waiting Coach chat aside (`coach_waiting[].words.set_aside` on Home): "not now", never an approval. Nothing is signed, answered or sent: its paused run ends, so Home's count and list drop it in the same read. kiff/agents can't cancel a run paused on a card (409 `paused_run`: a card has no KIFF exception to withdraw), so that run stays paused there, unanswered, until its card expires; tafs cloud keeps it as ended for 8 days, with the cards it waited on, and Home, set aside, Discard and the sweep skip it while it waits on those same cards. Answered in Coach after all, the run goes on, and a new card it pauses on shows. A connection one of its cards asked for, for an agent that's neither a live taf nor Coach, is withdrawn through the connection server's own end with the person's session, when the request carries their signed read (`Planque-Device-Signature`); without it, the connection waits out its own day. A sign-in or key another taf uses stays. KIFF's holds, "Did this happen?", currency asks and steps are never set aside this way. Answers `{set_aside: [session], withdrawn}`; the same again, by the same person, `{set_aside: [session], already: true}`, for 30 days; 404 for a chat that isn't theirs or doesn't wait. |
| `POST /v1/home/coach-waiting/set-aside` | a person | The same for every Coach chat of theirs that waits (`coach_waiting_clear` on Home, "Set all aside", sent for two or more, with `words: {button, title, text, yes, no}`: the title names the count it ends, "End 5 waiting Coach chats?", and the apps ask once). Answers `{set_aside: [sessions], withdrawn}`. |
| `POST /v1/home/setting-up/{kiff_agent}/discard` | the person who started that setup | Drops a taf being set up: the phone revokes the agent's Cards in KIFF first (`discard.cards` on Home), as before a removal, then asks this, signed as a removal is (`KIFF-Device-Signature` over `POST`, the path and the empty body, with the approval key; unsigned is still taken and logged `signed: false` until `TAFS_SIGNED_REMOVE=on`). tafs cloud proves it's the person's own half-made taf as Home does (a paused run of the account's Coach, started by them, whose result names that agent with the setup's own unclaimed draft), checks KIFF holds no Card in force on that agent any more, reading KIFF again a few times first since its read can trail the revoke just made (409 `cards_in_force` with `cards`, the ids still in force, otherwise: nothing ends while a Card could act), cancels that run (one that already moved on counts as ended), and forgets the name it kept, so Home no longer lists it. Answers `{discarded: true, kiff_agent}`; the same discard again, by the same person, answers `{discarded: true, already: true, kiff_agent}` (a phone may resend the request), for 30 days; 404 for any agent that isn't a taf being set up of theirs, including a handed-over taf and an improvement. |
| `GET /v1/tafs` | a person | The account's tafs the person may use or oversee, and removals not finished. Each taf's `next_run` comes with `next_run_words`, the next run in the person's language with its weekday when it isn't today ("Next run Mon 08:00"), for the apps to draw as is |
| `GET /v1/calendar?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` | a person | The calendar (kiff/tafs#534): the tafs' scheduled work still to come on those dates (inclusive, in the account's zone, at most 62 days; 400 past that or for a date that isn't one), for the tafs the person may use or oversee, as home lists them. `{time_zone, from, to, events: [{taf, name, color, kind, at, day, time, minute, words, count}], days: [YYYY-MM-DD], more, halted, words}`: `kind` is `schedule` (a live schedule's firing, read as kiff/agents fires it: its cron in its own zone, a wall time a DST change removes left out, a skipped firing left out, the month's last day where the schedule says so), `follow_up` (one the taf set itself) or `retry` (a run's next try); `at` is the instant (RFC 3339 UTC), `day`, `time` and `minute` its date, time of day and minutes since midnight in the account's zone, for the apps' slots, so neither app converts a zone; `words` what it is ("Every day at 09:00", "Try 2 of 3"); `days` the dates with an event, so the apps mark them. A schedule that fires more than 12 times on a day is one event that day, at its first firing, with `count` the firings it stands for and `words` saying from when to when; one read answers at most 2,000 events, and `more` says when some were left out. While the account is halted nothing runs, so it answers no events, `halted: true` and `words` saying so; a halted taf shows none beside running ones. Only what's to come: nothing past, nothing invented. A paused taf's schedules are off, so it has none; a taf whose schedules can't be read today shows none. Read-only; computed by tafs cloud so both apps draw the same |
| `GET /v1/record?q=&from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD&limit=&cursor=` | a person | The Record across the person's tafs (the ones KIFF says they may use or oversee, as home lists them), newest first, read from KIFF's activity with their session. `q` (at most 100 characters) finds a call by what it's about (its `about`, the taf's business reference such as "Order 1042", whatever its case or `#`), its action's name, or its amount: a whole number is every amount of it ("150" is 150.00 and 150.50), a decimal only that one. Never by a customer's name: KIFF keeps none. `{q, hint, entries, groups, next_cursor?, end_words?, none_words?}`: each entry is a taf's Activity entry with `taf {id, name}` and `about`; `hint` is the search box's words ("Search by order, invoice, amount or action"); `none_words` says nothing matched. A cursor belongs to its search, and a call made since refuses it (409: search again). ETag |
| `GET /v1/record/cases/{about}` | a person | One case: every call of the person's tafs about the same thing (`about`, URL-encoded, at most 120 characters; case, spaces and `#` don't count), oldest first, each with its receipt and how it went. `{about, steps, failed?}`: `failed {id, words, next?}` names the latest step that didn't go through, in words, and its own `next` (reconnect, try later, or Ask Coach with the step written in), which is the only retry: through the taf, under its Card, never a re-send from the Record. 404 when no call of theirs is about it, or when its calls are all older than the newest 2,000 the Record reads |
| `GET /v1/record/receipts?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` | someone who oversees or manages every taf | The receipts of those days (inclusive, in the account's zone, at most 92 days) for an accountant: `{from, to, time_zone, receipts, csv, missing}`. `receipts` are KIFF's signed receipts exactly as KIFF issued them, never rebuilt; `csv` lists every call, oldest first (`day, taf, action, about, amount, unit, outcome, receipt`), with a cell a spreadsheet would run as a formula written as text; `missing` counts calls whose receipt KIFF couldn't give just now, listed without it. At most 500 receipts, and at most 2,000 calls in the range (400 for either: export fewer days); 403 for anyone who doesn't see every taf's Record |
| `POST /v1/tafs` | `cards.manage` | Start a taf: `{name, draft_id + claim_secret \| from_setup + draft_id \| agent_key, summary?, kickoff, instructions?, cron + check \| unscheduled, tools?, timezone?, budget_cents?, notes?, recipe?}`. Its job is its own, as Coach drafted it from the owner's words (kiff/tafs#476): `summary` is what it does in one line (shown as its `job_summary`), `kickoff` what each run is asked to do (2,000 characters at most; the first run's too), `instructions` how it works (2,000 at most), and `cron` when it runs, or `unscheduled` when it works from its chat; a taf of its own with neither is refused. A taf of its own on a schedule checks its work first: `check: {tool, arguments, items, cursor, id?, first?}` names the read that lists its work, which each firing makes without the model, so the AI runs only when it lists something new (kiff/agents' schedule `check`). `tool` is one of the result's `tools`, `arguments` a plain object of at most 2 KB, `items` the dotted path to the list in the result (`""` when the result is the list), `cursor` a field that only grows, `id` the field that names an item, and `first` is `new` (the first check only takes the cursor) or `all` (it hands every item listed); left out, tafs cloud sets `all`. Without a check, or with one that doesn't fit, it's refused (400, in words Coach acts on); `check` with `unscheduled` or a starter job is refused too. The taf keeps it (`check` on its record), its schedule carries it, and its first run is its schedule fired once now (`POST /schedules/{id}/trigger`): the first check hands it the open queue, and a first firing that starts no run is still a success: `session: null, outcome, words, reason?`, as Run now answers it. A first check that couldn't read also reaches Coach: in the result's answer itself (`{taf_id, check: <the reason>}`, so its closing message never calls the taf working), and as a line with the reason it reads with the owner's next message, so it fixes the check with `taf_dial`. A kiff/agents that doesn't take `check` yet is answered `{taf_id}` alone, and the line still tells Coach. The taf keeps the result's `tools` (`tools` on its record), for Coach's dial checks. `recipe` is the id of the recipe it was built from (`GET /v1/recipes`), kept on its record (`recipe`) for its estimate's tests ([A taf's estimate](#a-tafs-estimate)); one that isn't a recipe's id is left out, never a reason to refuse the taf. A Coach result's `taf.recipe` is passed through the same way. `job` names one of the five starter jobs instead, with their kickoff, instructions and default schedule, for the apps from before ([tafs.md](tafs.md#its-job)); never with `from_setup`: a taf Coach sets up carries the job it drafted, and a starter job there is refused (400) **With no plan yet** (KIFF's balance says `kind: "none"`), the taf is made but no first run starts: the answer has `session: null` and `plan_needed: {words}` ("Choose a plan to start it.", in the person's language), which the "taf made" screen shows. Its first run is owed: it starts by itself once the account has a plan, at the next Home read or the 5-minute sweep, with no Run now. The same `plan_needed` is on a Coach result card's `answer.outcome` when tafs cloud's hand-over made the taf with no plan: tafs cloud keeps it, since kiff/agents records a result's answer with its known fields only. |
| `GET /v1/setup` | a person | `{setup, tools}`: the account's Coach (Setup), or `null`; and Coach's tools as KIFF has them. `tools.state` is `given` (every tool connected here and each of Coach's Cards in force; a revoked Card doesn't count), `to_give`, `taken` (`taken`: the name a vendor's tool holds; one of Coach's tools at tafs cloud's earlier address is connected again instead, its entry marked `replace: true` and its line saying it replaces the earlier connection) or `unknown`. The phone signs and sends each body as it is; the signed submit is the owner's one confirmation. **Before Setup exists, with `TAFS_COACH_START_ONE=on`,** someone who may start it also gets `start`: Coach's first start under one fingerprint. It holds `state`, `scheme` (`kiff.requests.v1` for the pair, `kiff.request.v1` for the draft alone), `agent_id` and `requests[]`, which are the tools batch (`connect`, for the gateway, with its lines) and then Coach's agent draft *with* its Cards (`draft`, for `POST /v1/me/agent-drafts`, with KIFF's own words for the Cards, which the gateway's `POST /v1/connections/batch/preview` gives for the pair, checking the batch as it would, and KIFF's draft preview gives for the draft alone), plus `coach_tools_step`. For the pair, the draft's body also carries the gateway's `contract_digest`, what the Cards were worded over, so it's signed with them and KIFF refuses the draft at submit if the tools changed since; a gateway preview that names none means no `start`. When KIFF can't word them, or anything fails on the way, there's no `start`, and the first start goes in its steps. The phone signs both with one `signed_at`, sends the batch first and the draft once the batch answered, then claims the draft's key with `POST /v1/setup` as before. With every tool already connected, `start` carries the draft alone: that's also the retry when KIFF refused the draft after the batch went in **A new account's time zone, from the phone:** the apps send the phone's zone as `X-Time-Zone`. While the account has no taf, KIFF's zone is still UTC, and tafs cloud has never set it, tafs cloud sets it in KIFF once (`PUT /v1/me/time-zone`, with the person's session) and answers `time_zone` with it. The apps then ask no time-zone question. A later mismatch is still theirs to ask. **`start.words`** `{lead, start, does}`: the one line before Start, its button, and what it does (approvals on this device and Coach's tools, with one face or fingerprint). The apps draw these instead of their own paragraphs, and with `start` there's no separate tools step: Start's own screen shows these words and, under them, Coach's Cards in KIFF's words (`requests[draft].card_title` and `cards`, with `coach_tools_step`'s details folded in), so the one tap signs nothing unread. |

#### Disconnecting and removed tafs

What the owner sees is in [connect-apps.md](connect-apps.md#disconnecting).

- **Disconnect** is the phone's call to the connection service, not tafs cloud's: its signed `DELETE /v1/accounts/{tenant}/grants/{id}`, under the owner's confirmation. The connection service removes that connection's tools from KIFF, and revokes its sign-in at the app only when no other live connection uses it (a second taf that reused it, or the same provider account). The last connection on a sign-in revokes it.
- **`removed_tafs`** names the removed tafs, so the apps never infer one. Each is a KIFF agent that KIFF has archived, as removing a taf does, and that is no taf of tafs cloud's any more (not live, not still being removed, not Setup), with when KIFF archived it (`removed_at`), newest first. A grant whose `request.agent` is one of these is from a removed taf, and the grant's `agent_name` names the taf. It's listed only for someone with `cards.manage`, who alone can clear. Anyone else gets `[]`, and so does everyone when KIFF's agents can't be read, so nothing is offered to clear on a guess. Tafs removed before removal archived their KIFF agent aren't listed, so their connections stay under Other connections in the apps.
- **What Clear keeps** is decided here too. With the phone's signed grants read (`Planque-Device-Signature`, as for a taf's Setup), each removed taf carries `grants`: its live connections (not `disconnected` or `failed`), each with `used_by`, the live tafs whose Card in force has a rule on one of the connection's actions. KIFF's tools are the account's, so a taf can draw on a connection another taf asked for. A connection's actions are its `tools` once it's connected, else each asked tool's name, leaving out Setup's own reads. Clear disconnects only those with an empty `used_by`. Without the proof, or when the connection service's grants or KIFF's Cards can't be read, a removed taf has no `grants`, and the apps offer no Clear on it (`removedTafs` and `tafsUsing` in `runtime/src/cloud/handler.ts`).
- **Removing a taf for good** disconnects every live connection its agent asked for, signed under the removal's own confirmation, after its Cards are revoked and before tafs cloud removes the rest. Its result says the connections are disconnected, or how many couldn't be.

Not found, it wakes the taf as any other event, and is kept ten minutes for a run that pauses on it right after (a vendor faster than the pause), answered from the run's pause note. Every N is 15, 20 or 30 minutes or whole hours, and a window starts and ends on the hour: anything else is 400, naming the nearest form. Answers `{version, words}`: the taf's next version, made as an improvement makes one, and what it changes in words. ## Logos

The apps show each app's logo, small and on a light tile, next to its name, and load it only from tafs cloud, so
no person's address reaches a third party (`logos.ts`).

- **Named:** when tafs cloud builds a view from planque's catalog (Connect apps, the gallery, the recipes), it
  records each logo's source, planque's `icon` (Composio's URL), under a key, the first 32 hex of the URL's
  SHA-256, kept 30 days after the catalog last named it. At most 100 a view; https on the default port, to a
  host name, only. The view carries `logo: "/v1/logos/{key}"`, a path, never a URL.
- **Served:** `GET /v1/logos/{key}` serves only a recorded key, so a phone can never name what tafs cloud
  fetches. The first request fetches the source, then it's kept 30 days. A key the catalog stopped naming
  expires with its record, and its logo isn't served any more.
- **Fetched:** https only, to an address that is public after the name resolves (private, loopback,
  link-local with the metadata service, shared, documentation and reserved ranges refused, IPv4 inside IPv6
  too; one such answer refuses the name), with no redirect followed, in 5 s and 256 KB at most, and only an
  SVG or a PNG. A source that fails isn't tried again for a day, and tafs cloud's log says why it failed
  (`logo_refused`, with the key).
- **Drawn:** Composio's logos are SVG, which neither phone draws by itself, so tafs cloud draws each as a
  96 px square PNG: the logo, fitted by its longer side into the middle three quarters, on a white rounded
  tile, so a dark logo reads on a dark screen and both apps show the same (resvg, as WebAssembly, in a
  worker thread stopped after 2 s). The standard public DOCTYPE line (`<!DOCTYPE svg PUBLIC "…" "…">`,
  with no internal subset) is dropped first; any other DOCTYPE or an entity, a script or foreign content,
  an href that isn't a fragment or an embedded raster image, a `url()` that isn't a fragment, a size over
  10,000 or a side 16 times the other is refused, and the renderer loads no font, file or image either way.
  A PNG source is checked (at most 1,024 px a side, 200 KB) and drawn on the same tile.
- **Bounded:** at most 300 new logos a day across accounts, so the store holds at most about 9,000.

## A taf's estimate

Before the owner approves a scheduled taf, or a dial version of one, Coach says what it will likely use
in a month, in one line tafs cloud writes from records (`runtime/src/cloud/estimate.ts`). Coach says it
as given and never a figure of its own. Every figure is credits as KIFF counts them: tafs cloud works
out no credits itself. A run here is a counted run that did work: a quiet check firing costs nothing,
since a schedule reads its work list without the model.

**Where the figures come from,** best first; `source` names the one used:
1. `own`: the taf's own counted runs over the last 30 days, from KIFF's record. Credits per run are the
   median and the 90th percentile of the runs that used credits. Runs a month are the count over the
   days the record covers × 30 / those days, once at least 7 days of history cover it: the pace.
2. `trial`: a new version's trial runs, which KIFF doesn't count. KIFF's record gives each the credits
   it would have used. Credits per run only: runs a month still come from the taf's own runs. KIFF's
   record doesn't name the version a trial ran, so every trial run in the 30 days counts.
3. `tests`: the same job's runs in our Evals, by the taf's `recipe`, read at runtime from tafs cloud's
   own store (the table Coach's state is kept in), under the key `estimates#v1`: a map of recipe id to
   `{p50, p90, runs, from}`, in credits. It's written from outside this repo, from the Eval reports, by
   whoever runs the Eval, with an expiry it sets (past it, the item reads as none, as any in that table);
   no figure is in this repo. Credits per run only, said as "in our tests". A
   recipe the store doesn't keep, a figure that isn't whole, or a read that fails is no figure: the line
   falls to the honest one.
4. `none`: nothing to go on, said so.

Runs that failed, or are free for any reason but a trial, are left out. A new taf's runs a month
aren't known before it runs: tafs cloud sees no sample of its work list at draft time.

**Where it reads them.** The route reads KIFF's runs for the taf with the person's session (`GET
/v1/me/usage/credits/runs`, this period and the one before). Coach holds no session of the person's,
so its tool reads them with its call's permit, as for `/v1/coach/act`: `POST /v1/coach/credits` with
`{permit, agent}` (the taf's KIFF agent), which answers `{runs: [{run, ended, credits, counted, free}]}`,
newest first, the runs that ended in the last 30 days. A counted run carries its counted credits; a
trial (`free: "trial"`) carries what it would have used. Any other answer, or none within 2 seconds,
leaves those sources out, a 404 from a KIFF without the route included: the line falls to our tests,
or says it can't tell yet.

**The words** say what the plan already covers before any euro figure, in the person's language (all
eight):
- inside the plan's included credits: "About … credits a month. On its own, it fits inside your
  plan's … a month.";
- Solo, past them: what the plan includes, then how many more. No euro figure is said on Solo;
- Company, past them: the credits past the plan's, their price at Company's published €0.01 a credit,
  and whether that stays within the account's spend limit from KIFF's balance, or goes past it;
- no pace: "I can't tell yet how often work comes in.", what a run used (so far, in its trial, or in
  our tests), and "After its first week, you'll see what it really uses.";
- nothing at all: "I can't tell yet what it will use." and the same last line. No figure is guessed.

The last line depends on the plan. Solo: "It never goes past your credits: when they run out, it
waits." Company: "It never goes past your spend limit: there, it waits." An agreement's plan
(`unbounded`), or no plan known, has none.

Credits are rounded to the nearest 10 (to the nearest 50 from 1,000), euros to whole euros. A taf is
held against the plan's included credits on its own.

**Coach's `taf_estimate`** (a read, on its own Card, `coach-estimates`, 200 a day) takes `{taf}` for an
existing taf, or a new taf's draft, `{recipe?}`, and `lang?`. It answers `{estimate: true, source,
words}`, or `{estimate: false}` when KIFF can't say what the person may see, or the whole tool takes
longer than 3 seconds: Coach then says nothing about cost and goes on. The plan is the account's last
balance read, which tafs cloud keeps two days (`tenant#plan`, [data.md](data.md)). No approval, result
or install waits on an estimate: nothing but this route, the tool, Plan & usage and the conversation
read below reads one.

**The week-one note:** one note in a taf's chat, once: "Refunds used 120 credits in its first week, so
about 510 a month at this pace." It's posted on the first read of the taf's conversation (`GET
/v1/tafs/{id}/conversation`) between 7 and 14 days after the taf started, by someone who manages it:
KIFF lists everyone's runs on a taf only to them. The read takes the taf's counted runs of its first 7
days from KIFF with that person's session, as the route does. Its figures are kept on the taf
(`weekOne`), so it's posted once; the thread shows it as a message with `note: true`, in the reader's
language. It's read beside the rest of the thread, at most a second and a half, and never fails it:
when it can't be read, the thread shows without it and a later read tries again. A first week the read
can't cover whole gets none, and a taf past its second week never does.

## Your step

A step a taf may never take itself, such as approving a bank transfer, signing, or submitting a form only its owner may, is prepared by the taf and handed to its owner with kiff/agents' `your_step` tool. KIFF keeps it as a hand-off: the step stays refused at the gateway, and the hand-off grants nothing. The run pauses on it as on a held call, by KIFF's hand-off id.

- **Home** lists the steps waiting for the person as `your_steps: [{handoff, run, taf: {id, name}, what, why, link?, link_host?, since, expires, words: {title, done, skip, open?, line}}]`, oldest first. Needs you counts each once (`needs_you.count`).
- <a id="coach-waiting"></a>**Coach waiting.** Home lists each Coach chat that's paused on a card its person answers (an approval, a sign-in, questions, a confirm), unanswered and not past its `expires`, as `coach_waiting: [{taf, session, run, since, words: {line, open}}]`, oldest first, one per chat. Only the person who started the chat sees it. `words.line` names the chat as Coach's history does, what it waits for and since when ("Set up refunds: Coach is waiting for your approval since 13 Nov.", or without a title "Coach is waiting for your approval."), `words.open` the way in ("Open Coach"), `words.set_aside` the swipe's ("Set aside", below); the apps open Coach's chat `session` on `taf`. Needs you counts each once (`needs_you.count`), so Home never says "Nothing needs you." while Coach waits. A setup's result card isn't one: that's `setting_up`. When the paused runs can't be read, none is listed or counted. A chat that has waited 48 hours ends on its own, and so does one whose card names a Card draft another run already made or a taf being removed (the sweep, `stale_coach`, which logs `ended: false, code: "paused_run"` for a run kiff/agents keeps paused, and doesn't end it again; a run kept that way is asked once more, since kiff/agents cancels a run paused only on cards: a cancel marks it done, anything else keeps it, `coach_kept_retry`); one paused on its result keeps its own Finish and Discard.
- **A taf's request** that waits on one has `your_step` with the same fields, and its `line` is the step's (`words.line`). The taf's summary is `needs_you`.
- **`link`** is passed on only as an https URL the taf's own connections vouch for: one of their hosts or a host under it, or a web host tafs cloud lists for that vendor (Stripe's dashboard for a Stripe connection), never another host on the same site or a shared platform (planque's list of the account's connections, read on Home only when a step waits). `link_host` comes apart, so an app never parses a URL to decide what to show. Any other link is left out, along with `words.open`, and so is every link when the connections can't be read: a taf that read a crafted message can't put a look-alike sign-in page behind the button. The app shows the words and no button. A taf's request view carries no link.
- **The words** are tafs cloud's, in the person's language: "Your step", "Done", "Skip it", "Open in {vendor}" (the vendor's name from the link's host, "Stripe" for app.stripe.com, else the host without `www.` or `app.`), and the line "Your step: {what}". The apps add none of their own.
- **The answer goes to KIFF**, not to tafs cloud: `POST /v1/me/handoffs/{handoff}/answer` with `{"answer": "done" | "skipped", "signature": {signed_at, value}}`. The phone signs it with its device key, over KIFF's `kiff.handoff.v1` message (tenant, hand-off id, answer, version, signed_at), as it signs a hold's answer. KIFF tells the taf's runtime through the alert target tafs cloud hands on (`handoff_answered`), and the run goes on: "Done" asks it to check the vendor where it can, and "Skip it" to withdraw what it prepared. No answer within KIFF's week is never done.
- **The phones that answer the account's holds hear about it twice at most:** once when it starts waiting ("Your step: {what}"), and once a day later ("Your step is still waiting: {what}"). Each push opens the taf and carries `handoff`. The sweep finds them with one read per account a pass, kiff/agents' `GET /runs/paused?tool=your_step` (the account's paused runs, from its own paused list), never one read per taf.
- **Off until `TAFS_YOUR_STEP=on`:** a taf's definition gets `your_step: true` only then, once kiff/agents offers the tool and KIFF serves hand-offs, and the upgrade applies it.

## Paged Activity and setup views

`GET /v1/time-zones?q=` returns `{zones: [{id, title}]}`, from the service's IANA list,
searched on the service. Selecting a zone still uses KIFF's signed
`PUT /v1/me/time-zone`; tafs cloud never signs for the person.
After KIFF confirms it, `POST /v1/account/time-zone` (no body) applies the zone
KIFF reports to every recurring schedule of this account's tafs. It requires
`cards.manage`, accepts no zone or account from the caller, and returns
`{time_zone, updated}`. It preserves the schedules' cron, pause, busy and check
settings. Absolute one-off times and follow-ups do not move. A partial failure
returns 503 with words explaining that the account changed but some schedules
need retry; repeating it updates only schedules still in another zone.
New tafs use the account's zone when no explicit zone was supplied at creation.
Home tafs carry `time_zone`, `time_zone_words` and `schedule_time_zone`, so the
apps can name the account's clock and identify a schedule still using another.
`schedule_time_zone_words` is present only for that mismatch and gives the next step
in words. A failed sync sends its sentence in both `error` and `words`. The cloud
rechecks KIFF's zone after schedule writes: if another confirmed zone overtook the
request, it applies the newer one before reporting success; repeated changes or
unfinished writes return the retryable 503.

`GET /v1/tafs/{id}/activity?filter=all&limit=20&cursor=…` returns `entries`, day
`groups`, `filters: [{id, title}]`, the selected `filter`, `card_unit_exceptions`, a `revision`, and an
optional `next_cursor`. Filters are `all`, `done`, `held`, or `refused`, applied
on tafs cloud before a page is made. Held means a call waiting for an owner's answer; an allowed call still sending or with an unknown result stays in All. An allowed call with an unknown tool outcome
is never labelled done. Entries carry their ready-to-draw `title`, `detail`,
`time`, `day_title` and optional `amount_text`, plus KIFF's original call and
signed evidence. `tools` (comma-separated action names) and `from` (an ISO date)
can narrow an app's seven-day Activity. The server supplies that query on the app's
setup view; the phones do not identify apps from tool names.

A connection refusal is typed by KIFF as `tool_outcome.status: refused_by_tool`,
`refused_by: connection` and a machine `code`. Its `detail` is drawn as supplied,
or composed from that code if absent. Text prefixes never establish provenance.
A vendor's refusal shows the words KIFF recorded for it, quoted as the app's:
`The app refused it: "Agent Mail refused it (it answered 403): Forbidden."`, or
"The app refused it and gave no reason". Those words never decide whether
anything was done. The call's status, amounts, approval flags, receipts and
counting remain KIFF's unchanged evidence.

A `not_done` entry may carry `next`, what the owner can do about it
(kiff/tafs#476, gap 10): `{kind, words, tool, taf?, message?}`.
- `reconnect`: a vendor's 401 or 403, or the connection's `operation_changed`.
  The app's button opens that connection, the one whose `tools` include `tool`.
- `ask_coach`: a 400, 404, 405, 410 or 422, or the connection's `not_granted`,
  `definition_invalid`, `definition_unread`, `bad_arguments` or
  `permit_invalid`. The button opens Coach on the taf with `message` written in.
- `try_later`: a 429 or a 5xx, KIFF's `not_sent`, or the connection's
  `halt_unknown`, `record_unavailable`, `permit_expired` or
  `profile_not_applied`. `words` is a line, with no button.

The status comes from the "answered NNN" in KIFF's recorded words, as KIFF's
gateway reads it. An app draws `words` as a line for a `kind` it doesn't know.

A page is 20 entries by default, 1–50 when specified. Pass `next_cursor` unchanged.
It is bound to the account, person, taf and filters. A changed snapshot returns
409: refresh from the first page rather than joining inconsistent pages. A page
can be empty with a cursor when a bounded scan has not reached a matching call yet.
Only the final page supplies `end_words` ("Nothing older in this view"). Empty
intermediate pages keep their cursor and never claim that history has ended.

`GET /v1/tafs/{id}/work/runs?limit=20&cursor=…` pages the recent retained runs as
`requests`, with a `next_cursor` when another page remains. A preview requests
`limit=3`. This view reads the runtime's latest 100 runs; it is not an archive of
all past runs. The older `/runs` report endpoint is unchanged. Both new page
routes supply `end_words` at the end of their available history; for runs it
says "Nothing older in the retained runs" to make the retention boundary clear. The first page also
has `skipped`, as `GET /v1/tafs/{id}/work` gives it, when a recent firing started no run (a Run now
whose check couldn't read included): it was no run, and Its runs says so. Both page
routes send an `ETag`; repeating a read with `If-None-Match` returns 304 with no
body when unchanged. Apps keep previously read pages per account, taf and filter.

`GET /v1/tafs/{id}/setup` returns:

- `connections`: each app's display `name`, `action_count`, `action_words`,
  `actions` (each with its title, Card-limit lines and workflow-step numbers),
  the app's `steps`, `running`, and `stats` for the last seven days. Stats include
  `calls`, `done`, `held`, ready-to-draw `words`, `complete`, and an `activity_query`.
  Incomplete source reads are explicitly marked; they are never shown as a complete total.
- `workflow`: its `title`, `summary`, numbered `steps` with app/grant and running
  state, and `running_step` only while that step's tool call has no result. A step's `words`
  is empty when it would only repeat its `label`, so the apps don't show the title twice.
- `memory`: its note `count`, `title` and newest note as `latest`.
- `versions`: `Changes to how it runs`, the live version, earlier count and summary.

Optional `card_unit_exceptions` on Home and Setup, and the always-present array on Activity, contain the same display rows from
KIFF's `GET /v1/me/card-exceptions?status=held`, filtered by the visible taf's subject.
Each is `{id,kind:"card_unit",taf,name,title,words,history,action_title,coach_text,
dismiss_title,can_approve_once:false}`. `id` is KIFF's exception id; history carries
its count and first/last attempts. The Coach action opens that taf with the proposed
unit limit; it changes no Card. Dismiss re-reads the current KIFF exception and signs
its ordinary `reject` answer against the current accounting version. Approve once is
never offered for this kind. Ordinary holds remain separate, and every refused
decision remains in the Record. KIFF owns deduplication and closure when the action's
Card gains the currency or a call in that currency is allowed. An unreadable KIFF
response fails the read instead of claiming no exception waits.

Activity collapses the refused calls in a still-open currency episode beneath that
exception, matching only KIFF's typed `amount_unit_mismatch` reason, subject, action,
observed currency and episode times (or first proposal id). Other decisions and past
episodes remain visible. The original Record is unchanged. The panel is read afresh
with each page, independently of the history cursor revision: changes to its count or
closure do not discard older pages. Apps draw the latest page's panel metadata.
An empty `card_unit_exceptions` array clears an older row. If exceptions cannot be read
or their data is unreadable, Activity still returns its decisions with an empty panel
and `card_unit_exceptions_notice`; it does not claim that no requests wait. Normal
Activity failures still fail the page. Home and Setup retain their existing read errors.

The phone forwards one signed read of planque's account grants using the
`Planque-Device-Signature` header, signed for planque's `GET
/v1/accounts/{tenant}/grants`. tafs cloud forwards that proof unchanged; it does
not hold a device key, sign or approve anything. A missing or refused proof yields
`connections_error`; an unreadable connection list is not presented as an empty one.
A connection whose sign-in the vendor no longer takes (`needs_reconnect`) also gives the page a
`connect_again`: `{grant, app, title, words, done_words}`, the taf's own **Connect again**, in the reader's
language ("Gmail's sign-in has expired, so Rae can't use it. Connect again to sign in."). The app calls the
connection server's approve for that `grant` (as Connect apps' Sign in again does), so the same connection is
signed in again with its tools and the taf's Card, and shows `done_words` once it's connected. It never sends
the person to a list of every app.

`GET /v1/tafs/{id}/memory?all=true` returns all shared notes and this person's own
notes, newest first, plus their `summary`. The older request without `all=true`
keeps its 100-note limit. Forgetting a shared note still requires managing the taf.

## This phone's alert choices

`GET /v1/push` with `Tafs-Device-Token: <this phone's token>` returns `enabled`,
`sound`, `kinds: [{id, title, words, enabled}]`, and `answering: {title, tafs}` for
the per-taf results link. It reads only a registration belonging to the signed-in
person on this account. Tokens are never displayed or included in request logs.

`POST /v1/push` also accepts `kinds`, an object with boolean choices for
`needs_you`, `halted`, `answered` and `not_sent`. Missing choices default on; a token
refresh omitting `kinds` preserves that person's previous choices. They are
checked before either push provider is called. Disabled kinds count as handled,
so retries cannot bypass an opt-out. Background synchronization still clears
previously shown holds. Turning off the whole phone removes its registration.

`DELETE /v1/push` takes `{device_token, platform, environment}` and removes this
person's matching phone, idempotently, leaving other phones and the account's
KIFF alert secret in place. The older `DELETE /v1/push/{token}` is unchanged.

Each setup connection also carries `status_words` from planque (or cloud's fallback)
and `reconnect`; `custody_words`, one line on who holds the sign-in, as planque says
it (the connection service, or tafs' own), left out when planque doesn't say; and
`on_demand: true` when the app's sign-in was set up on the owner's pick rather than by
an operator (kiff/planque#87); and `account_words`, which vendor account it works in and
in which mode, planque's `account_name` and `mode_words` in one line ("Demo Shop ·
Live", "… · Mode not known yet: treated as live"), the name cut to 60 characters, left
out when planque says neither. A connected browser connection (planque's `provider: "browser"`)
also has `site_sign_in: {title, words}`: the action the apps offer to open planque's live sign-in
view (kiff/planque#86). A held sign-in stays visible with zero usable actions until its
actions are approved. Signed-out connections retain their source status and can
open the existing device approval and vendor sign-in flow; the cloud never signs
that approval.
