What to test in this build:

1. Languages. tafs now speaks 40 languages. Set your phone to one (Settings, General, Language & Region), open tafs, and tell us any line that is still English or reads oddly. Arabic and Hebrew read right to left.

2. Signing in. The welcome's tick and Continue open KIFF's sign-in in a sheet inside tafs, with Apple, Google, GitHub and email with a password. It should never leave tafs for Safari.

3. Sign in with a code sent by email. Account, Sign-in: it is Off unless you turn it on. Turning it on opens KIFF's sign-in page, where you confirm it's you; turning it off happens in the app.

4. Chrome. Account, Chrome: Pair a Chrome shows a code with a Copy button, to type into the tafs extension in Chrome on your computer. Only if you see Chrome in Account.

5. Needs you and Home, as in the last build: nothing is approved or sent when you set a waiting chat aside.

Tell us anything that reads oddly, or any screen where you weren't sure what to do next.
