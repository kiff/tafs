What to test in this build:

1. Coach's steps, live. Start a new taf with Coach. While Coach works, the chat says what it's doing ("Checking your Stripe connection…", "Drafting the Card…"), and Coach's words appear as it writes them.

2. A taf's Card, from any conversation. In Coach, ask what one of your tafs may do. Coach reads that taf's Card itself, says which Card covers live actions and which only test mode, and never sends you elsewhere to look.

3. Packs and Solo, with test purchases. In Settings, open Usage, then Add tasks, and buy a pack; on the trial, subscribe to Solo. Purchases in TestFlight are test purchases: on a test account they add tasks; on any other account the app finishes the purchase, adds nothing, and says "Test purchases don't add tasks to this account."

4. Asking an admin. As a member who can't add tasks, ask for a pack with a reason. As the admin, you're alerted: open Usage and approve it by buying the pack, or decline it with a note. The member is told the answer.
