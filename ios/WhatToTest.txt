What to test in this build:

1. The plan in sight. Settings opens with a Plan card: one line with your plan and the credits left ("Solo · 260 credits left", or "Solo · waiting: this period's credits are used" in amber). Tap it: it opens Plan & usage. Home shows one line about the plan only when it needs you: most of this period's credits used, or your tafs waiting for credits.

2. Credits. Plan & usage shows this period's credits used, each taf's credits and runs, and the runs that weren't counted (Coach within its fair use, trials of a new version, runs that failed on our side). Each run counts once, when it ends, in whole credits. Credits from packs never expire and are used after the period's own. Packs aren't sold on iPhone and iPad until the App Store carries them.

3. New conversation in Coach always works. On a conversation that's already empty it stays there and shows its top; on one with messages it opens a new one.

4. Signing out asks first. Sign out of KIFF, Switch account, and Sign in again (when this device can't approve) each ask before going ahead. Cancel leaves you signed in.

5. Your exported chats leave with you. Export my chats in Account, then sign out: the exported files are gone from the device.
