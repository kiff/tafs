What to test in this build:

1. Needs you. When Coach is waiting on you, Needs you lists it in plain words. Swipe a waiting chat away, or tap Set aside, to put it off; with two or more, Set all aside asks once. Nothing is approved or sent when you set one aside.

2. Home's first line. It now says how many tafs are working or ready, as tafs cloud counts them, in your language.

3. Connect apps. Each app is listed once, by its own name, with a short line under it saying what it can do. On iPad the App column keeps its room, so names no longer break mid-word.

4. Signing in to an app. An app's first sign-in says its Card comes next. A sign-in that didn't connect says so in plain words, never as a status code.

5. Devices. Account › Devices names each device in the app's language ("tafs on iPad").

Tell us anything that reads oddly, or any screen where you weren't sure what to do next.
